# Compensation rules for client damage

Applies to: managers and employees (for information). Template — adapt to your company before approving.

## Approval limits

How much compensation can a manager approve? Up to $60, without the director. Larger amounts follow
the table below.

| Damage amount | Who approves the compensation |
|---|---|
| up to $60 | the manager approves without the director |
| $60 to $400 | the operations director approves |
| above $400 | an insurance claim is filed; the director informs the client |

## Rules

- An employee never promises compensation or an amount to the client. Only the manager or the
  director tells the client the decision.
- The client is paid within 10 business days after the decision.
- Compensation is paid by the company. Deductions from an employee's salary are made only where the
  labour law allows it, never on the spot.
- Repeated damage by the same employee within 90 days leads to a training session with the team lead.
