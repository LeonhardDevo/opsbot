# Chemical safety

Applies to: everyone who works with cleaning products. Template — adapt to your company before approving.

## Never mix products

Never mix bleach (chlorine products) with acid cleaners, ammonia or any other cleaning products. The
mixture releases toxic gas. Use one product, rinse the surface with water, then use the next one.

## Protection

- Wear nitrile gloves for all cleaning products; wear goggles for oven and drain cleaners.
- Ventilate the room for 10 minutes: open the window before and after using strong products.
- Dilute products exactly as written on the label. More product does not clean better.

## Storage

Keep products in their original labelled containers. Every spray bottle must carry a label with the
product name and the date it was filled. Safety data sheets are kept in the folder in the van.

## First aid

- Skin contact: rinse with running water for 15 minutes, remove contaminated clothes.
- Eye contact: rinse with running water for 15 minutes, then call your manager and go to a clinic.
- Report every exposure in the bot as an incident with category "Injury".

Pregnant employees may refuse work with strong acid and chlorine products without any explanation.
