# No access to the property

Applies to: all cleaners. Template — adapt to your company before approving.

## How long to wait if nobody opens the door

1. Ring and knock. If nobody opens the door, wait 15 minutes at the property.
2. Call the client twice: when you arrive and again after 10 minutes of waiting.
3. Send the client the "No access" message from the reply templates.
4. Take a photo of the door with the apartment or house number as proof of the visit.
5. Report it in the bot as an incident with category "No access".

## What not to do

- Do not ask neighbours or the concierge to let you in.
- Do not leave notes or keys in the mailbox.
- Do not leave before the 15 minutes of waiting are over, even if the client does not answer the phone.

## Billing

A visit without access after the full waiting time counts as a late cancellation under the client
contract. The office, not the cleaner, talks to the client about the charge.
