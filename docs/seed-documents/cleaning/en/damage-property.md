# Client property damage: what to do

Applies to: all cleaners and team leads. Template — adapt to your company before approving.

## What to do if you scratched or broke a client's item

1. Stop work in that room. Do not try to repair, glue or polish the item yourself.
2. Take three photos of the damage: a wide shot of the room, a close-up, and a close-up with a coin
   or ruler next to it for scale. A scratched table, a cracked tile and a broken vase are all damage.
3. Tell the client calmly and politely what happened. Do not admit fault and do not promise any
   compensation or amount. Say: "I have recorded the damage and my manager will contact you today."
4. Report the incident in the bot within 30 minutes: press "Create incident" and choose the category
   "Damage". Attach the description of the item, the room and the time.
5. Continue the visit only if the client agrees.

## Broken glass or a broken mirror

Wear gloves, collect the shards into a box, tape it and write "Glass" on it. Never put shards into a
plastic bag.

## What happens next

The manager calls the client within 2 hours and decides on compensation according to the
"Compensation rules". The employee is never asked to pay for the damage on the spot.
