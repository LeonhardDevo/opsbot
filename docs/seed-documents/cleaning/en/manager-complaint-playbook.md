# Manager playbook: complaints and incidents

Applies to: managers only. Template — adapt to your company before approving.

## Response times

- Damage and quality complaints: call the client within 2 hours after the incident is created.
- Injury of an employee: call the employee immediately and inform the operations director the same hour.
- Missing items: contact the client within 4 hours.

## Handling the incident

1. Take the incident in work in OpsBot so the team sees who owns it.
2. Collect the photos and the employee's description before calling the client.
3. Use the compensation approval limits; anything above your limit goes to the operations director.
4. Close the incident only after the client confirms the resolution in writing.
5. Seven days after closing, send the client a short follow-up asking about the quality of the next visit.

## Coaching

Discuss every damage incident with the employee within 3 working days. Focus on the cause, not on blame.
