# Lost and found items

Applies to: all cleaners. Template — adapt to your company before approving.

## Valuables found during cleaning

- Jewellery, cash, documents and gadgets: leave them where you found them, take a photo and tell the
  client in writing. Never put valuables in your pocket, even to keep them safe.
- Items under furniture: place them on a visible surface and mention them in the visit report.

## Items left in the van

Hand them to the dispatcher within one working day. The dispatcher logs them in the lost and found
register; items are stored for 30 days.

## The client says something is missing

Do not argue and do not search the client's belongings. Create an incident with category "Lost item"
in the bot and describe the visit. The manager contacts the client within 4 hours.
