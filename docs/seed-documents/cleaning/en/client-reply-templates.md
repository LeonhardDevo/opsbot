# Client reply templates

Applies to: all cleaners and team leads. Template — adapt to your company before approving.

Use these messages as written; replace the words in angle brackets.

## Running late

If you are running late by more than 15 minutes, message the client before the start time:
"Hello, this is <name> from <company>. I am running late and will arrive at <time>. I apologise for
the delay."

## No access

"Hello, this is <name> from <company>. I am at your door for the cleaning booked at <time>, but nobody
opens. I will wait until <time + 15 minutes>. Please call me if you are on your way."

## Quality complaint

If the client complains about quality within 24 hours after the visit, offer a free re-clean within
48 hours: "We are sorry the result did not meet your expectations. We will re-clean the areas you
mention free of charge within 48 hours. Which time suits you?"

## Damage noticed

"I have noticed damage to <item> and recorded it with photos. My manager will contact you today to
resolve it."
